Privacy Policy

How Campus2Air collects, uses, shares, and protects your information.

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

Overview

Campus2Air is a carpool matchmaking platform for UC Riverside students coordinating shared rides to and from airports. This Privacy Policy explains exactly what we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, and what rights you have over it.

We are not a consumer app, a data company, or an advertising platform. We operate this service as a student project. We collect only what is necessary to make carpools work.

Campus2Air is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by the University of California, Riverside or the Regents of the University of California. UCR does not receive your Campus2Air data from us and is not responsible for this service.

1. What We Collect

Account Information

  • Your UCR Google account email address (@ucr.edu only), collected via Firebase Authentication (Google Sign-In).
  • Your name, taken from your Google account when you sign in. We store your first name and last initial.
  • Your phone number, which you enter when you create or join a carpool.
  • A record that you confirmed you are 18 or older and agreed to the Terms of Service: the version you agreed to and when. Signing in is not possible without both.

Carpool Data

  • Flight code, airport, date, trip direction, planned time, seat count, and any optional notes you enter.
  • Flight numbers riders choose to add when joining a carpool. These are stored with the carpool as a list, not with your account, and are not shown next to anyone's name.
  • Records of carpools you have joined or created.

Safety Reports

  • Reports you send about another carpool member, reports others send about you, and what the administrators did about them (including any suspension).

Activity and Notifications

  • In-app notifications about your carpools (for example, someone joined or a carpool was disbanded). These are not marketing emails.
  • An activity log of what happens on the service (see Section 5).
  • Our hosting provider automatically records standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the time of each request, for security and troubleshooting.

We do not collect location data, payment information, or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other websites.

2. Why We Collect It

Every piece of data we collect has a single purpose: operating the carpool matching service and keeping it safe. Specifically:

  • Your email signs you in and confirms that you have a UCR-issued Google account. It does not confirm that you are currently enrolled, who you are, or that you are safe to travel with.
  • Your name and phone number let carpool members recognize and coordinate with each other.
  • Your carpool details power search and matching and are shown to other signed-in users.
  • Reports, the activity log and server logs help us investigate abuse and respond to safety problems.

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any purpose beyond running the carpool service.

3. Who Can See Your Information

  • All signed-in users can see: your first name and last initial, and your carpool's flight numbers, airport, date, trip direction and time.
  • Members of your carpool can also see: your phone number and the carpool's notes. Notifications sent to members use only your first name and last initial.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • Anyone with an invite link (and the link previews messaging apps show) can see a short carpool summary without signing in: route, date, time and seats left. It never includes names, phone numbers, notes or flight numbers.
  • Campus2Air administrators can see: your email, name, phone number, carpools, Terms acceptance, the activity log, and reports, so they can run the service, investigate reports and handle requests. A report you send is not shown to the person you report.

We do not sell, rent, license, or share your personal information with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or any external parties for commercial purposes. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect someone's safety.

4. Third-Party Services

Campus2Air relies on the following third-party infrastructure providers to function. Each is bound by its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google): handles authentication (Google Sign-In). Google's Privacy Policy applies to authentication data.
  • Vercel: hosts the application. Vercel's Privacy Policy applies to server-level access logs.
  • TiDB Cloud / MySQL: stores carpool data in a secured database.

We do not control the data practices of these providers beyond the scope of our service agreements with them. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Retention

  • Carpool listings are automatically deleted at 11:59 PM UTC on the day after the listed date. Their membership records and flight lists are deleted with them, or earlier if the carpool is disbanded.
  • A record of which carpools you joined is kept for up to 48 hours to enforce the limit of 2 joins every 24 hours.
  • The activity log is kept for up to 180 days and only administrators can see it. It records events such as signing up, logging in, and creating, joining, leaving or sharing a carpool, linked to your email. It also stores the free-text reason an organizer gives when removing a member or disbanding a carpool, and the email of the person who removed someone or handed over a carpool.
  • In-app notifications are deleted after 90 days.
  • Your account (email, name, phone number and Terms record) is deleted automatically after 1 year without signing in, if you are not in any carpool. You can ask us to delete it sooner (Section 8).
  • After a carpool ends, a summary without names, emails, phone numbers or notes (flight code, airport, date, seat count, group size and whether it departed or was disbanded) is kept for usage statistics.
  • Safety reports and suspension records are kept for as long as needed to keep the service safe and to handle legal claims, including after your account is deleted.

You may leave a carpool or disband a carpool you organize at any time.

6. No Background Checks

Campus2Air does not perform background checks, identity verification beyond requiring a UCR-issued Google account, or screening of any kind. Matching is based solely on flight and schedule data.

By using Campus2Air, you acknowledge that you are meeting other people at your own discretion and risk. We are a coordination tool, not a vetting service. If someone makes you feel unsafe, use the Report option on My Carpool, and in an emergency call 911.

7. Data Security and Cookies

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including HTTPS (encrypted connections), secure database access controls, and server-side session management.

Cookies. We use one necessary session cookie, which keeps you signed in for up to 30 days after your last visit and protects your actions from forgery. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Signing out removes your sign-in from that browser. Your browser also stores your light/dark theme choice, and which Terms version you agreed to on that device (so sign-in can finish after Google redirects you), in its local storage.

Do Not Track. Some browsers send a “Do Not Track” (DNT) signal. Because we do not track you across websites or use your data for advertising, we treat all visitors the same and do not change our practices in response to DNT signals.

No online service is fully immune to security risks. We recommend using a strong, unique password for your UCR Google account, which is your primary credential for this service.

8. Your Rights (Including California / CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and we offer the same to every user:

  • Right to know: You may request a description of the personal information we have collected about you and how it has been used, and a copy of it.
  • Right to deletion: You may ask us to delete your personal information.
  • Right to correct: You may ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Right to non-discrimination: We will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.
  • Right to opt out: We do not sell or share personal information for advertising. There is nothing to opt out of.

How to make a request. Email us at {% if legal_contact_email %}{{ legal_contact_email }}{% endif %} from your @ucr.edu address. We confirm the request by replying to that address, then respond within 45 days as required by California law. For a deletion, we remove your account, phone number, notifications, join history and activity-log entries, and take you out of your carpools (carpools you organize are closed and the riders are told). We keep safety reports and suspension records, usage summaries that contain no personal information, and anything the law requires us to keep. Please do not post requests or personal information publicly.

9. Age Requirement

Campus2Air is only for people 18 and older, and you must confirm that you are 18 or older to sign in. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 is using the service, contact us and we will delete their account.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of Campus2Air after any changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

We will not make retroactive changes that reduce your rights without clear notice.

11. Contact

Questions, data requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy can be sent by email to {% if legal_contact_email %}{{ legal_contact_email }}{% endif %}. Please do not post requests or personal information publicly (for example, in the project's code repository).
